# Indirect competitors: what makes year-in-review content shareable

Research date: 2026-11-26. Scope: notable "Wrapped", "Year in Review", "Recap" and "Year in Sport"-style products, mostly the 2024 and 2025 editions. Claims are cited inline. Anything I could not confirm from a primary source is marked **[unverified]**. Where I relied on a search snippet and could not open the page, that is noted too.

Gaps: no verified data for Monzo, Revolut, Google Photos Recap, Tidal's 2025 edition, Xbox (no official Microsoft recap found), Instagram's global year-in-review, TikTok, Vercel/Linear/Notion/Figma/Raycast/Arc, LeetCode, and Reddit's 2025 Recap details. The Spotify 2025 share-card specs, the X card dimensions and the Instagram Story safe zones are also weaker than the rest (see the platform specs section).

---

## TL;DR

1. **Spotify Wrapped is the template, and it is data-driven.** Its 2025 edition added Listening Age, Clubs, Wrapped Party, a Fan Leaderboard and an AI "Listening Archive". Spotify says 200 million users engaged in the first 24 hours and content was shared 500 million times (self-reported) ([Spotify newsroom 2025](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2025-wrapped-user-experience/), [IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.com/record-breaking-spotify-wrapped-2025-sees-200m-engage-500m-shares-day-3792059)).
2. **Identity labels with a small set of named types win.** Spotify's Clubs (six groups), YouTube Recap's personality types (14 per [TechCrunch-level coverage](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/02/youtube-releases-its-first-ever-recap-of-videos-youve-watched); count differs by source), and Uber's YOUBER (14 profiles, per [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2025/12/15/uber-launches-youber-year-in-review-for-us-users)) all turn stats into a character a person can post.
3. **Novelty formats travel further than dashboards.** Receiptify (a shop receipt of your top songs) and Instafest (a festival lineup poster) both went viral on formats, not on data depth ([Scotsman](https://www.scotsman.com/read-this/heres-how-people-are-making-receipts-of-their-top-spotify-tracks-2981151), [TechCrunch on Instafest](https://techcrunch.com/2022/11/28/instafest-app-lets-you-create-your-own-festival-lineup-from-spotify/)). Amazon Music's 2025 Delivered is built around a festival too ([Techloy](https://www.techloy.com/amazon-music-drops-2025-delivered-recap-with-festival-posters-alexa-stats-and-new-listener-badges/)).
4. **Backlash came from generic or AI-written output, not from privacy.** The 2024 Spotify edition was criticised for an AI podcast, missing features and stats that fans thought were AI-made ([TechCrunch on the AI podcast](https://techcrunch.com/2024/12/04/spotify-wrapped-2024-adds-an-ai-podcast-powered-by-googles-notebooklm), [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/audio/spotify-wrapped-2024-controversy-explained-what-you-need-to-know)). Spotify's 2025 edition "made it less about AI" per [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/12/04/spotify-wrapped-2025-less-about-ai/), though some fans still called it "AI slop with paid stats" ([Headphonesty, search snippet only](https://www.headphonesty.com/2025/12/fans-slam-spotify-wrapped-ai-slop-stats/)).
5. **Self-roasting and "Be Nice / Roast Me" modes add shareability.** Receiptify offered an AI "Be Nice" or "Roast Me!" option, per [Heavy](https://heavy.com/?p=2888639) (search snippet). Roasting content is cheap to share because it lets the subject laugh at themselves.
6. **Percentiles and rarity are a flex, but only when the base is clear.** YouTube shows a rarity percentage per personality type (e.g. Adventurer at 5%) ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/02/youtube-releases-its-first-ever-recap-of-videos-youve-watched)). Spotify's Fan Leaderboard shows rank by minutes without a stated percentile ([Spotify newsroom](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2025-wrapped-user-experience/)). Steam Replay compares you with the average Steam user ([Steam Replay coverage](https://www.engadget.com/gaming/pc/steam-replay-2025-is-here-to-recap-your-pc-gaming-habits-205430951.html)).
7. **Eligibility thresholds are a built-in tease.** Spotify needs 30 songs streamed over 30 seconds and five artists ([Spotify newsroom](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2025-wrapped-user-experience/)). Letterboxd needs ten diary entries ([Letterboxd coverage](https://www.academicjobs.com/global-news/letterboxd-wrapped-2025-takes-over-social-media-movie-stats-viral-trends-33), weak source). Discord may hide Checkpoint for low-activity users ([Discord coverage](https://discord.com/blog/checkpoint-2025-discord-year-in-review)).
8. **Shares are most often screenshots or saved images.** Strava's in-app recap is hard to share and users save images themselves ([Strava support](https://support.strava.com/en-us/articles/15401959-your-year-in-sport)). Nintendo's scorecard is a downloadable image ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/gaming/nintendo/the-nintendo-switch-2025-year-in-review-is-finally-here-145810913.html)). Build the image-first output.
9. **Timing matters.** Most editions land in the first week of December (Apple Music Replay 2 Dec, Amazon 2 Dec, YouTube 2 Dec, Spotify 3 Dec 2025). Steam Replay (16 or 17 Dec), Strava (8 Dec to 23 Jan) and Discord (from 4 Dec) also landed in December. Letterboxd shipped on 2 Jan 2026 and Nintendo in mid-January, so later launches are possible. Being early or late is a choice, not a default.
10. **Developer recaps exist and go viral in small circles.** GitHub recap tools (Remotion's GitHub Unwrapped, third-party "Wrap" tools) and Cursor's "Year in Code" page (cursor.com/2025) were shared on forums and blogs ([Cursor forum](https://forum.cursor.com/t/share-your-year-in-review/147241)). No primary engagement numbers were found for these, so treat their reach as small and unmeasured.

## Deeper notes on the 8 most instructive

### 1. Spotify Wrapped (2024 and 2025)

**Mechanics.** The 2025 edition has a "Classics" set (minutes, top songs, artists, genres, podcasts) and a "new" set. The new set includes Listening Age (compares your most-played release years with listeners your age), a Top Song Quiz, Top Albums, audiobook and author/podcaster clips, Top Artist Sprint (monthly ranking changes), Fan Leaderboard (rank by minutes among an artist's listeners, no percentile stated), Clubs (six listening-style groups), and a Listening Archive of up to five AI-generated reports on memorable days, English only in select markets ([Spotify newsroom 2025](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2025-wrapped-user-experience/), fetched).

**Social mechanics.** Wrapped Party lets you invite up to nine friends, and awards update each time you join ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/12/03/spotify-wrapped-2025-new-features), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/03/spotifys-2025-wrapped-becomes-a-multiplayer-experience)). This turns a solo stat into a group event, which is the strongest social change in the 2025 edition.

**Share format.** Each data story has a share card. Users can send it through Spotify Messages or post it to social channels ([Spotify newsroom 2025](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2025-wrapped-user-experience/)). Aspect ratio and card dimensions are not stated in the sources I could open.

**Viral hook.** Listening Age was widely discussed and meme-able. Spotify says 200 million engaged users in 24 hours (up 19% on 2024) and 500 million shares (up 41%), including screenshots and downloads. These are self-reported ([IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.com/record-breaking-spotify-wrapped-2025-sees-200m-engage-500m-shares-day-3792059)).

**Criticism.** 2024 was the low point. Fans said the data was missing (top genres and Audio Aura were dropped), the AI podcast was disappointing, and some made-up genre names such as "Pink Pilates Princess Strut Pop" were read as proof the stats were AI-generated. Spotify said no generative AI was used for Wrapped results or design ([Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/audio/spotify-wrapped-2024-controversy-explained-what-you-need-to-know), [The Week](https://theweek.com/culture-life/spotify-wrapped-a-slave-to-the-algorithm), [search summaries](https://www.salon.com/2024/12/04/why-spotify-users-are-complaining-that-spotify-wrapped-is-sucky-this-year)). Spotify said 2024 Wrapped was still its biggest first-12-hours day, up 26% on 2023 (per Fast Company, reported in a search summary; **[unverified at source]**). The 2025 edition was framed as a response: Fortune reported executives said the 2024 disappointment "fueled" this year's update and that it was "less about AI" ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/12/04/spotify-wrapped-2025-less-about-ai/)). Headphonesty still ran "AI slop with paid stats" in 2025 ([search snippet](https://www.headphonesty.com/2025/12/fans-slam-spotify-wrapped-ai-slop-stats/)).

**Lesson.** Real, specific stats beat generated text. When fans suspect the numbers are invented, the whole thing falls apart. Show the source of every number.

### 2. YouTube Recap (2025)

**Mechanics.** Up to 12 cards covering top channels, interests, how habits changed, and a personality type. There are 14 personality types according to TechCrunch-level coverage; another outlet says "at least 11" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/02/youtube-releases-its-first-ever-recap-of-videos-youve-watched)). Each type shows a rarity percentage (e.g. Adventurer 5%) ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/02/youtube-releases-its-first-ever-recap-of-videos-youve-watched)).

**Design process.** YouTube says it ran nine feedback rounds and over 50 concept tests, and an animator gave each character its own movement style ([YouTube blog, snippet only; page returned 500 on fetch](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/unpacking-youtube-recap-2025-personality/)). This is a useful model for the character design work: a small set of named characters with movement and personality.

**Lesson.** Named characters with a rarity figure give people something to be and something to compare. Keep the list short.

### 3. Uber YOUBER (2025)

**Mechanics.** Combines rides and Uber Eats. Stats: overall rating, total rides, destinations, preferred ride types, ordering habits. Each user gets one of 14 personality profiles based on "YOUnique habits" ([Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2025/12/15/uber-launches-youber-year-in-review-for-us-users)). The longest ride (about 700 miles, Austin to Pensacola, around 11 hours) was highlighted ([Uber coverage via search](https://www.uber.com/us/en/blog/you-moved-the-world-in-2025/)).

**Share format.** Shareable cards opened from a home-screen banner ([search summary](https://www.boston25news.com/news/trending/uber-launches-youber-2025/JK3YAW776NFSTCLZ73H5UY72VI/)).

**Viral hook.** The "embarrassing Eats orders" angle, and an SNL sketch spoofing Uber Eats Wrapped ([AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/snl-spoofed-uber-eats-wrapped-235836787.html)). A mainstream comedy show parodying the format is a sign the format is recognised.

**Lesson.** Embarrassing-habit stats (here, late-night food orders) are the humour people repost. Transferable to AI coding as "embarrassing prompts" or "most-retried prompt".

### 4. Receiptify (2020, fan tool)

**Mechanics.** Your top 10 songs, rendered as a shop receipt with itemised entries. Inspired by the Instagram account @albumreceipts ([Scotsman](https://www.scotsman.com/read-this/heres-how-people-are-making-receipts-of-their-top-spotify-tracks-2981151)). An "AI analyze" option offered "Be Nice" or "Roast Me!" ([Heavy, search snippet](https://heavy.com/?p=2888639)).

**Why it spread.** Four reasons, from the sources:
- A familiar, recognisable format (a receipt) that needs no explanation.
- It works off-season. You do not have to wait for Wrapped to get your stats ([Scotsman](https://www.scotsman.com/read-this/heres-how-people-are-making-receipts-of-their-top-spotify-tracks-2981151)).
- Shared taste becomes connection: people find others with the same music ([Scotsman](https://www.scotsman.com/read-this/heres-how-people-are-making-receipts-of-their-top-spotify-tracks-2981151)).
- Roast mode makes the share funny rather than just a flex.

**Caveat.** It runs on a third-party host and needs Spotify access. Verify its current status before pointing people at it.

**Lesson.** A familiar physical object as the frame is the cheapest route to a viral image. A receipt for your AI coding year (itemised sessions, a total at the bottom) is a direct analogue.

### 5. Instafest (2022, fan tool)

**Mechanics.** Sign in with Spotify or Last.fm; the app reads recent listening and generates a festival-style poster. Premium adds poster styles and artist picks. A "niche-ness" score is part of the poster ([TechCrunch 2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/11/28/instafest-app-lets-you-create-your-own-festival-lineup-from-spotify/), [routenote guide](https://routenote.com/blog/?p=94209)).

**Scale.** A secondary summary reports more than 5 million posters within a few days of launch **[unverified; not from a primary source]**.

**Why it spread.** A festival poster is a status object: it says your taste is worth headlining. The format is a poster, so it reads well on a phone story.

**Lesson.** A "lineup" frame turns a list of tools or models into a flex. "Headliners: the models you ran most; Undercard: the MCP servers you tried once."

### 6. Steam Replay (2025)

**Mechanics.** Games played, most-played games, genres, achievements, longest play streaks and monthly playtime. Comparison with the average Steam user. A profile badge is unlocked on completion ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/gaming/pc/steam-replay-2025-is-here-to-recap-your-pc-gaming-habits-205430951.html), [dlcompare](https://www.dlcompare.com/gaming-news/steam-replay-2025-arrives-with-a-full-gaming-recap-and-a-collectible-badge-68557)).

**Share format.** A shareable summary image, with visibility set to public, private or friends ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/how-to-get-steam-replay-2025)).

**Viral hook.** Self-deprecating stat moments. PC Gamer's headline was about spending half its gaming time in Bongo Cat, and the reporter "regrets nothing" ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/steam-replay-2025-is-live-i-spent-half-my-gaming-time-this-year-in-bongo-cat-and-i-regret-nothing)). Steam's own headline stat was that about 14% of playtime went to games released that year (per PC Gamer's coverage).

**Lesson.** One ridiculous top-line stat ("half your gaming went to a rhythm cat toy") is what gets quoted. Build the headline stat first, then the rest.

### 7. Discord Checkpoint (2025)

**Mechanics.** Your messages sent, voice time, most-used emoji, top server, top friend, five most-played games. At the end you get one of ten Checkpoint cards ([Discord blog](https://discord.com/blog/checkpoint-2025-discord-year-in-review), fetched). Platform-wide: 744 billion messages, over 200 million monthly users per coverage ([Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2580679/discord-launches-checkpoint-and-reveals-744-billion-messages-in-2025-year-end-recap)).

**Share format.** Optional share of a summary embed into chat. Sharing is opt-in and the default is private ([Discord blog](https://discord.com/blog/checkpoint-2025-discord-year-in-review)).

**Design decisions worth copying.**
- Each card comes with a matching avatar decoration, and the page frames it as a way to find people with the same card ([Discord blog](https://discord.com/blog/checkpoint-2025-discord-year-in-review)). This is a social-matching hook built into the design.
- Opt-in sharing and a clear privacy line: the blog says it uses activity data, not message content ([Discord support, snippet](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/36415877391511)).
- Eligibility: hidden for low-activity users and when personalisation is off ([Discord blog](https://discord.com/blog/checkpoint-2025-discord-year-in-review)).

**Lesson.** Pair a card with a visible matching cosmetic, and make the share explicit and opt-in. Privacy-first is also a trust signal.

### 8. Strava Year in Sport (2025): the paywall lesson

**Mechanics.** A personal recap in the app. Requires the latest app version and an active subscription. Mobile-only ([Strava support](https://support.strava.com/en-us/articles/15401959-your-year-in-sport)). Available from 8 Dec 2025 to 23 Jan 2026 ([Strava support](https://support.strava.com/en-us/articles/15401959-your-year-in-sport)).

**Sharing.** Strava says only you can see it on your Progress page. Users save images or videos themselves, and the 2025 version dropped several items compared with 2024 ([Strava support](https://support.strava.com/en-us/articles/15401959-your-year-in-sport); a user write-up in the search results). A paywall-gated recap, with no share button, is a clear counter-example.

**Backlash.** Ars Technica reported the recap was put behind an $80 paywall ([Ars Technica via Harvard TagTeam mirror](https://tagteam.harvard.edu/hub_feeds/3415/feed_items/17132945/content)). The price figure comes from that report; I did not open the Ars original.

**Lesson.** Do not gate the share artifact. The recap spreads when it is free to make and free to post.

## Patterns that make wrapped content shareable

1. **Identity flex.** Named types, clubs, personas and ranks. Spotify's Clubs, YouTube's personality types, Uber's 14 profiles and Discord's ten cards all give a named identity to post. Each is a short list.
2. **Self-roast.** Roast Me mode (Receiptify), "embarrassing Eats orders" (Uber), "half my gaming time in Bongo Cat" (Steam). A person posts a joke about themselves faster than a brag.
3. **Percentile and rarity bragging.** Only where the base is clear. YouTube's rarity percentage per type, WakaTime's "top 3%", Cursor's "top 0.1%" posts. The base (who is compared to whom) must be stated, or the number reads as fake (see the Spotify 2024 backlash).
4. **Novelty format.** Receipt (Receiptify), festival poster (Instafest, Amazon Music), trading card and personality card (YouTube, Discord, Uber). A format the viewer already understands needs no explanation.
5. **Comparison with friends.** Wrapped Party (Spotify 2025), Discord's matching cards, Steam's comparison with the average user, Receiptify's "find people with the same taste". The social layer raises sharing rates.
6. **Scarcity and timing.** Limited windows (Strava 8 Dec to 23 Jan; PlayStation 9 Dec to 8 Jan) and late-year launch. Early December is a crowded window; being in it is a choice.
7. **Eligibility as a tease.** Spotify (30 songs over 30 seconds, five artists), Discord (activity threshold), Twitch (10 hours watched). A missing result becomes a reason to use the product more.
8. **Headline stat first.** One stat that is surprising or silly carries the share: "half my gaming time in a rhythm cat toy", "longest ride 700 miles". Lead with it on the card.
9. **Image-first output.** Most shares are saved images or screenshots (Strava, Nintendo, Steam). The share artifact is the product.
10. **Trust is a feature.** Opt-in sharing, source-of-data notes (Discord: no message content), and no fabricated numbers. The 2024 Spotify backlash shows that generated stats destroy credibility.

## Humour patterns and personality text

**What landed.**
- Specific, slightly embarrassing facts: "longest ride 700 miles, 11 hours" (Uber), "half my gaming in Bongo Cat" (Steam), "top 0.1% of Cursor users" (forum posts).
- Named, memeable characters: YouTube's Sunshiner and Skill Builder, Spotify's Club Serotonin and Soft Hearts Club, Uber's personality profiles.
- Roast modes, with the user opting in.
- Ridiculous but true-feeling labels: Spotify's Listening Age memes (per coverage; no count found).

**What got called cringe or worse.**
- Generic stats that could have come from any user (Spotify 2025 detractors, per [search snippets](https://www.headphonesty.com/2025/12/fans-slam-spotify-wrapped-ai-slop-stats/); the Fortune piece reported fans said the stats did not match their year).
- Invented-looking data (Spotify 2024 genre names).
- AI podcasts that tried to be witty about the user and missed (Spotify 2024; a commentator called the year "full of AI garbage", per [search summary](https://www.tomsguide.com/audio/spotify-wrapped-2024-controversy-explained-what-you-need-to-know)).
- Jokes that touched on a sensitive topic, such as a Spotify One Direction remark that critics called distasteful ([AOL, search snippet](https://www.aol.com/spotify-criticised-distasteful-one-direction-161601139.html)).

**Rules that follow.**
1. Every joke must be traceable to a number the user can check.
2. Roast the habit, not the person. Use tool and process comedy ("you said 'fix the one typo' 47 times").
3. Keep generated text short and optional. The 2024 Spotify AI podcast was long and people did not want it.
4. Avoid comparisons with real people or groups that could read as mocking.
5. Let users edit or regenerate the line before sharing.

## Idea bank: 34 funny or LARP-able formats for AI coding logs

Each idea needs a real stat behind it. Where a stat needs an assumption, the assumption is stated. No idea below requires a number this research has verified for the tool.

**Character and identity**
1. **Trading card.** Name, class (Bard for docs-heavy, Wizard for refactors, Paladin for test writers), rarity from your tool mix, "Special move" = your most-used command.
2. **Six Clubs.** Mirror Spotify's Clubs with six coding-style groups (e.g. "Plan-First Monks", "YOLO Raccoons", "Diff Readers").
3. **Relationship status with Claude.** "It's complicated" when the reprompt rate is high; "Committed" for long sessions; "Single" when the last month had no sessions.
4. **Claude's diary about you.** First-person entries generated only from session facts, with a visible "source: 14 sessions" footer.
5. **Coding-age.** Compare your tool habits to eras (copy-paste from chat = "2023 energy"; subagents and hooks = "2026 energy"). Mirror Spotify's Listening Age, but do not claim a real population baseline unless one exists.
6. **Horoscope.** Star sign from your median session start time; a daily-style prediction for next year built from your own stats.
7. **Mood ring.** Color from session length and interruption rate.
8. **Patch notes for your year.** A game-patch-notes format listing what got buffed (tools) and nerfed (abandoned tools).

**Receipts and lists**
9. **Itemised receipt.** "1x Fix the one typo (47 attempts)". Total at the bottom. Mirrors Receiptify.
10. **Festival lineup.** Headliners = top 3 models; Main stage = top tools; Undercard = MCP servers tried once. Mirrors Instafest and Amazon Music.
11. **Tier list.** Your tools ranked S to D by time saved (user-chosen, not measured).
12. **Graveyard.** Repos started and abandoned after 3 or fewer sessions, with epitaphs.
13. **Hall of fame.** Your top 5 most reused prompts, with their call count.

**Awards**
14. **Most apologised-to bug.** The bug that took the most sessions to fix.
15. **Longest single session.** With a joke about the hour.
16. **Rage-quit award.** Sessions ended within 60 seconds of a tool error (define the threshold).
17. **Undo champion.** Most reverts, rewinds or git resets.
18. **Yes-man award.** Number of AI changes accepted with no visible edit. Needs careful wording to avoid shaming users.
19. **Night-owl index.** Share of sessions after midnight.
20. **First-time achievements.** First subagent, first hook, first MCP server, first commit by the agent.

**Humour from the model's behaviour**
21. **"I apologise" counter.** Count of model apologies in the year.
22. **Hallucination hall of fame.** Times the model cited a function, flag or file that did not exist (only if detectable; say so).
23. **Confidence score.** Ratio of "I'm sure" statements to later corrections.
24. **Politeness index.** Count of "please" and "thanks" in your prompts.
25. **Swear-o-meter.** Profanity count directed at the model or a bug.

**Honest equivalents (use stated ranges)**
26. **Tokens as pages.** "Your prompts and outputs filled about N printed pages." State the tokens-per-page assumption.
27. **Water and energy.** Present as a wide range with the method in a footnote. Data-centre water and energy per token are uncertain; show both the range and the source of the coefficient, or skip the number.
28. **Cost as a car or coffee.** Convert your API spend to "about N months of coffee", using the price on the day, and say which price table.
29. **Lines of code as books.** "About N novels' worth of lines" with an assumption about words per line.

**Progress and comparison**
30. **Top-x% framing.** Only with a real, disclosed base (e.g. "top 3% of your own team"). Otherwise skip.
31. **Stack Sprint.** Monthly ranking of your top tool, like Spotify's Top Artist Sprint.
32. **Wrapped Party for devs.** Friends compare anonymised stats in a shared room, with awards that update as members join.
33. **Fan leaderboard.** Your rank among users of one tool, only with real, disclosed data.
34. **Plot twist.** The project that changed direction the most, shown as a timeline.

**Bonus**
35. **Sidekick.** Your agent as a named sidekick with its own stat card, written from its logs.
36. **Pixel sprite.** A generated avatar matching your class, like Bitmoji and Discord's avatar decorations.

## Shareability notes for the tool

- Make the story card (1080 x 1920) the hero. Use a headline stat, one character card and one roast line.
- Give every card a visible data source line ("from 214 sessions, Jan to Dec").
- Make sharing opt-in and private by default. Discord's model is the cleanest example.
- Do not gate the share. Strava's paywall shows the cost.
- Ship a roast mode and a "be nice" mode. Receiptify shows both get used.
- Add a "matching card" hook so people look for others with the same class, as Discord does.
